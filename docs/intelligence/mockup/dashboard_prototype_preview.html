Reasons
• Charging to 60% by 05:30 keeps the battery above 45% (your 40% reserve plus a 5% margin) until the next cheap window, even if demand runs high and solar low. The expected-case need is 55%.
• Before the cheap window opens at 00:30 the battery is predicted to fall to 33% - below your 40% reserve. The target above cannot fix that: it needs an earlier top-up or less discharge.
• On the pessimistic path the battery would reach your 40% reserve at about 21:00 before the cheap window opens, needing about 5.8 kWh of peak-rate import. This is a risk to watch, not something the overnight target can change.
• Without grid charging the battery would reach 40% at about 23:00.
