Reading the table
- pass = checks held, excluding finding pins. A finding pin (pins) is a held check named 'finding-reproduces:<id>' that
  confirms a recorded finding still reproduces exactly; it is printed as PASS but counted only under pins. A FINDING is a
  reproduced deviation from an invariant and is never a pass: a suite exits 0 when every check holds and each of its findings
  reproduces exactly as recorded.
- finding counts each finding once across the lab; a duplicate of another finding (in any suite) is counted under its owner
  only, and a merged variant prints no FINDING line and is recorded under its owner. UNSUPPORTED marks a construct the lab
  meets but does not model: uns_ids counts the distinct catalogue ids a suite printed, uns_pts the points (one printed line
  each; several points can share one id). blocked counts scenarios that could not run. phase counts executed checks tagged
  phase-sensitive. tz is yes when zoneinfo (Europe/London) was usable in that suite's process; when it is no, that suite's
  zoneinfo cross-checks were printed as skipped.
- runtime_s is the suite's own LAB-SUMMARY runtime and wall_s the runner's view; with --parallel 4 the suites shared the CPU,
  so these runtimes are contended and are not judged against a per-suite budget.
